## AI Contribution Policy

You must fully understand and be able to explain anything you submit. Signing
off on a commit certifies under the [Developer Certificate of Origin][dco] that
you have the right to submit it under this project's licences (see
[LICENCE](LICENCE.md)); you cannot make that certification for work you do not
understand or whose origin you cannot account for.

{{ if .Scaffold.includeAIPolicy -}}
All contributions are subject to the [AI Usage Policy](AI_POLICY.md). All AI
assistance must be disclosed, and AI tools are not authors: commits that credit
an AI tool in a `Signed-off-by` or `Co-authored-by` trailer will be rejected.
{{- else }}
Any contribution (bug report, feature request, or pull request) that uses
undeclared AI output will be rejected.
{{- end }}
